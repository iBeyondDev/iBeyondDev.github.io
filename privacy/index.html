Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all games and applications ("Services") developed and published by iBeyond (Mohammad Bani Khaled) ("we", "us", or "our") on Google Play and other app stores. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This page explains what information is collected when you use our games, how it is used, and the choices you have.

By downloading or using any of our games, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account or directly provide personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number to play our games.

However, when you use our games, some information may be collected automatically by us or by the third-party services integrated into our games:

  • Device information — such as device model, operating system version, language, screen size, and unique device identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID).
  • Usage data — such as gameplay progress, session length, in-game events, crash logs, and general interaction with the game.
  • Approximate location — a coarse, non-precise location (such as country or region) derived from your IP address, used for analytics and to serve regionally appropriate ads. We do not collect precise GPS location.

2. Third-Party Services

Our games may use third-party services that collect information used to identify you. These services have their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob
  • Google Analytics for Firebase
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Unity (Unity Analytics & Unity Ads)

Not every game uses all of these services. The services actually used may vary from game to game.

3. How We Use Information

The information collected is used to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve our games;
  • Fix bugs, diagnose crashes, and optimize performance;
  • Understand how players interact with our games (analytics);
  • Display advertisements, including personalized ads where permitted;
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

4. Advertising

Our games may display third-party advertisements (such as banner, interstitial, or rewarded ads) served by networks like Google AdMob or Unity Ads. These networks may use your device's advertising identifier to serve ads, measure ad performance, and — where you have consented — show personalized ads.

You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time through your device settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization (or "Delete advertising ID" on newer Android versions).

5. Children's Privacy

Our games are intended for a general audience and do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). Where our games are directed at or accessible to children, we configure our advertising and analytics partners to comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the GDPR provisions on children's consent, and the Google Play Families Policy — including serving non-personalized ads where required.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete such information.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Automatically collected data is retained only as long as needed for the purposes described above, after which it is deleted or anonymized. Data collected by third-party services is retained according to their own policies.

You may request deletion of any data associated with your device by contacting us at the email address below. You can also reset or delete your device's advertising identifier at any time from your device settings.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example, the European Economic Area under GDPR, or California under CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you;
  • Request correction or deletion of your data;
  • Object to or restrict certain processing;
  • Withdraw consent to personalized advertising at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

8. Security

We value your trust and use commercially reasonable measures to protect the information processed through our games. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Links to Other Sites

Our games may contain links to external sites (for example, our social media pages or app store listings). If you follow such a link, you will be directed to a site we do not operate, and we strongly advise you to review its privacy policy. We have no control over, and assume no responsibility for, the content or practices of third-party sites.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. You are advised to review this page periodically. Changes are effective immediately after they are posted.
